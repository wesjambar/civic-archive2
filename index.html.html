About this project

Knowledge before opinion.

The Civic Archive is an independent educational project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or any government agency. Its purpose is to make civic information accessible, encourage the use of primary sources, and present historical and institutional topics with care. Where possible, readers are directed to original documents and official public resources.
